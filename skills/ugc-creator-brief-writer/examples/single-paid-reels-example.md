# Single Paid Reels Brief (Spark Ads) — Reference Example

Fictional brief used to show structure. Never reproduce verbatim.

**Product:** River Stone Ceramic Pour-Over
**Brief type:** Paid UGC (the buyer will boost this as a Spark Ad on Meta)
**Platform:** Instagram Reels
**Creator:** One creator, single brief

---

## What's different from the organic brief

Same product, same brand voice, same hook archetypes. What changes:

1. **FTC disclosure language is required** — both in-video and in-caption
2. **Usage rights paragraph is included** — explicit license for the buyer to run as paid
3. **Brand safety section is included** — preempts ad-rejection triggers
4. **Hooks lean slightly more conversion-focused** (less moodier "slow Saturday" framing, more clear value-proposition framing)
5. **B-roll shot list is tighter** (6 shots, all conversion-supporting — fewer wildcard shots)
6. **Don'ts include the ad-rejection triggers** (personal attributes, health claims, etc.)
7. **Deliverables include approval flow** (buyer signs off on raw before ad goes live)

---

## Output (.docx) structure highlights

The Overview, Talking Points, and Timeline sections are similar to the organic brief. Below are the sections that change for paid.

This license does NOT include:
- Use of your name, likeness, or content outside the platforms named above
- Use of your content in print, TV, OOH, or any non-digital channel

You retain ownership of the underlying creative work and may continue to use it
on your own channels indefinitely.

Compensation: $750 flat fee (covers production + 3 months Spark usage)

══════════════════════════════════════════════════
BRAND SAFETY — PLEASE AVOID
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

These trigger Meta's auto-rejection on paid ads. Following the list keeps the ad live:

- Saying or implying the viewer has a body type, condition, or attribute (Meta's
  policy bans this even when framed positively). Use "if you want X" instead of
  "if you're [type of person]."
- Medical or health claims about the product, even mild ones ("clears," "stops,"
  "fixes," "doctor approved"). Use "designed for" or "may support."
- Profanity, even bleeped. Meta sometimes auto-flags the bleep itself.
- Mentioning other platforms ("Follow me on TikTok," "Subscribe to my YouTube").
  Meta downranks cross-references in paid content.
- Before/after body shots, weight references, transformation timelines.
- Casual references to addiction, crisis, mental health, war.
- Implying medical effects from drinking the coffee — keep claims about the
  product (it's a ceramic dripper) not about caffeine's effects.

If your video gets rejected by Meta's auto-review, we'll lose the ad spend
and have to scrap the content. Following the list above keeps us both in
the clear.

══════════════════════════════════════════════════
HOOKS (slightly more conversion-focused for paid)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

[The 10 hooks, with the contrarian and curiosity-gap ones slightly tempered
toward concrete value props that earn ad clicks. E.g., the "Espresso machines
are over" contrarian becomes "Espresso machines collect dust — this gets used
every morning."]

══════════════════════════════════════════════════
B-ROLL SHOT LIST (6 shots — tighter for paid)
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

[6 conversion-supporting shots; the more wildcard / atmospheric shots from the
organic brief are dropped. Every frame should be paying its way for an ad.]

══════════════════════════════════════════════════
DELIVERABLES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Total videos: 1 + approval workflow
Instagram Reels: 30s, 9:16

Approval flow:
1. Deliver the raw clips + your initial edit to our Frame.io link
2. We review and provide notes within 48 hours
3. You incorporate feedback and deliver the final edit
4. Once approved, post to your @[creator_handle] account with the
   Paid Partnership tool enabled
5. We boost the post as a Spark Ad

══════════════════════════════════════════════════
DO'S AND DON'TS
══════════════════════════════════════════════════

Do
- Include the FTC disclosure in the first 2 seconds of the video (text overlay)
- Use Instagram's Paid Partnership tool when posting
- Stick to licensed audio (Reels has a library for paid use) — no trending sounds
- Send us raw clips + your edit so we have material for cutdowns
- Mention the product naturally; the conversion-led hooks above already do the
  lifting

Don't
- Don't post until we've signed off on the edit
- Don't use trending TikTok / Reels audio that isn't in the Commercial Music Library
- Don't make health, medical, or transformation claims about coffee or the
  product itself
- Don't say "best pour-over"
- Don't reference any other coffee brand by name (TOS + legal risk)
- Don't cross-promote other platforms in this Reel ("follow me on TikTok" etc.)

══════════════════════════════════════════════════
TIMELINE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════
Shoot by: May 26
Deliver by: May 28 (raw + initial edit)
Approval round: May 28-30
Final edit due: May 31
Post by: June 2
How to deliver: Frame.io link sent separately.

## Notes on this exemplar

- **FTC disclosure language is exact** — buyer is protected legally, creator knows precisely what to put on screen
- **Usage rights paragraph is concrete** — duration (3 months), platforms (Meta/TikTok/YouTube), cutdowns allowed (5), what's excluded (print, TV, OOH, beyond 3 months)
- **Brand-safety bullets preempt the ad-rejection triggers** — saves the buyer from a rejected ad and a wasted shoot
- **Hooks are slightly more conversion-tuned** than the organic version, but still voice-aligned
- **Approval flow is built into deliverables** — creator knows the buyer reviews before the ad goes live
- **Don'ts include the paid-specific things** (no trending audio, no cross-platform promo, no health claims) on top of the universal brand-voice things
- Compensation ($750) is concretely stated — no awkward "to be discussed later"
