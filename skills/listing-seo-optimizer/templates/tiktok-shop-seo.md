# TikTok Shop SEO

TikTok Shop's discovery algorithm is video-first: most product views come from creators posting videos that tag the product, not from search. But TikTok Shop *does* have a search surface (the Shop tab and in-app search), and the algorithm uses listing-level signals when surfacing products in the For You feed of Shop browsers.

The optimization levers are different from every other marketplace: hashtags, attributes, and short attention-grabbing copy carry the weight.

## Ranking factors (in rough order of impact)

1. **Product attributes** — category, material, size, color, target audience. Powers the recommendation engine.
2. **Hashtags** (where supported) — TikTok-native discovery signal.
3. **Product Name keyword match** — limited weight, very short field.
4. **Description keyword presence** — moderate weight.
5. **Video associations** — products tagged in viral videos rank higher in browse. Out of scope for this skill but worth flagging.
6. **Conversion rate** — TikTok Shop heavily rewards listings that convert (impulse-purchase platform).
7. **Recency** — newer products get a slight boost.

## What this skill optimizes

### Product Name — hard cap 34 chars
- Yes, 34. This is a real constraint.
- Front-load the product noun + one differentiator.
- Skip the brand name unless the brand IS the hook.
- No emoji in product name (some categories flag).
- Example: "Velvet Hair Claw Clip 4-Pack" (28 chars) — concrete and scannable.

### Product Description — hard cap 800 chars
- First 100 chars are above the fold on mobile — make them earn the scroll.
- Open with use case or transformation, not a feature list.
- Short paragraphs (2-3 lines max). Line breaks > walls of text.
- Emoji used sparingly is platform-native — 1-2 per listing, not per line.
- Voice should feel like a real person who likes the product. Polished agency copy reads as inauthentic on TikTok.

### Product attributes — fill every category-specific attribute
- TikTok's recommendation engine relies heavily on attribute matching to surface products in the right For You feed.
- Material, size, color, target audience (age, gender, style), use case — fill them all.
- Mis-categorization on TikTok is much more punishing than on Amazon because the wrong For You audience won't convert and the algorithm will down-weight further.

### Hashtags (where shown)
- TikTok Shop hashtags work like TikTok video hashtags. Short, scrollable, niche.
- Mix: product type hashtag, use-case hashtag, audience hashtag, trend hashtag (if a trend genuinely applies).
- Don't use hashtags that don't apply just to chase volume — TikTok's algorithm flags mismatched hashtags as spammy.

## Bullet-equivalent for TikTok Shop

TikTok Shop doesn't use bullets. For scoring purposes, evaluate the description's structural quality: does it open with a hook, use short paragraphs, have a scannable rhythm?

## Completeness checks (TikTok Shop-specific)

- Product Name 28-34 chars (utilizes the slot)
- Description ≥300 chars (out of 800)
- All category attributes filled
- 4+ product images (TikTok Shop allows 9)
- At least one video associated with the product (huge ranking lever — flag this if missing)
- Hashtags filled where the platform allows

## Common pitfalls

- **Product Name over 34 chars** — truncates mid-word on mobile.
- **Long paragraphs** — TikTok readers don't scroll walls of text.
- **Polished agency-style copy** — reads as inauthentic.
- **Empty attributes** — recommendation engine surfaces the product to wrong audiences.
- **No associated videos** — product effectively invisible. Flag for buyer.
- **Repeating Product Name in the description opener** — wasted real estate above the fold.

## Rewrite output structure for TikTok Shop

```
TIKTOK SHOP — Rewrite

Product Name (XX/34): [...]

Description (XX/800):
[POV/transformation opener]

[short paragraph: what it is + key feature]

[short paragraph: how it's used or who it's for]

[1-2 emoji sprinkled in, max]

Attributes (enter in TikTok Shop Seller Center):
- Material: [...]
- Size: [...]
- Color: [...]
- Target Audience: [age, gender, style]
- Use Case: [...]
- [other category-specific attributes]

Hashtags (where shown):
#producttype #usecase #audience #trend

Video association note:
- Associated videos: [current count]
- Recommendation: [if 0, flag that this is the highest-leverage next step for TikTok Shop ranking]
```
