# Platform: TikTok

The fastest-growing platform for product seeding and creator partnerships. Different from Instagram: more casual, more video-native, creators expect product samples to test/film with before agreeing to deliverables.

## Channel options

- **TikTok DM**: most common
- **TikTok Creator Marketplace** (for brand-registered partnerships): more formal, contract-driven
- **Email** (if in bio or LinkTree): preferred for mid-tier + macro
- **Comment** (warm replies only)

TikTok DMs have **lower open rates than Instagram** because creators get fewer brand DMs (yet) but the platform's DM UX is less prioritized.

## Length

| Tier | DM length |
|---|---|
| Nano | 60-100 words |
| Micro | 80-130 words |
| Mid-tier | 100-150 words |
| Macro | use email or TikTok Creator Marketplace |

TikTok creators prefer shorter messages than Instagram. Compress the brand intro to one phrase.

## Tone

TikTok culture is even more casual than Instagram:
- **Nano**: very casual ("hey! 👋"); emoji are native; sentence fragments fine
- **Micro**: casual professional
- **Mid-tier**: professional but lighter than IG-equivalent
- **Macro**: still casual relative to other platforms

The biggest mistake: outreach that reads like email-style copy. TikTok creators respond to TikTok-native voice.

## Personalization

Reference specific videos — not just topics. TikTok creators measure their content in individual videos, not "themes."

Example:
> "Hey Marcus — your video this week about the espresso-vs-pour-over taste test cracked me up (the chocolate-milk reference KILLED). Wondering if you'd test our pour-over kit and see if it changes your verdict."

Note: "this week," "the taste test," "the chocolate-milk reference" — three specific signals. The opener proves you actually watched.

## Product sample expectation

TikTok creators almost always need product in hand before deciding to film. The skill defaults to "we'll send the product first, you film if you love it" framing for nano/micro/mid-tier.

This is different from Instagram (where some creators will accept gifted-with-expectation-of-post) and especially different from YouTube (where contracts often pre-commit deliverables).

## Required disclosures on TikTok

- **Gifted only**: "thanks to [brand] for sending this" verbally + #gift in caption + TikTok's "Branded content" toggle on
- **Hybrid / Paid**: TikTok's "Branded content" toggle MUST be on; #ad and #partner in caption
- **Affiliate**: #affiliate + commission disclosure verbally

TikTok's branded content toggle is non-optional for paid partnerships — the platform actively enforces.

The skill includes the toggle expectation in outreach so creators know.

## Outreach structure (TikTok-specific)

```
[Casual specific opener referencing recent video — 1-2 sentences with personality]

[Brand intro compressed to 1 phrase — "we make pour-over coffee gear" not a paragraph]

[Why the product fits THEIR style of content — 1 sentence]

[Offer — "send you the kit, no strings" or "$X + product" — 1 sentence]

[Soft ask — "would you wanna try it?" — 1 short sentence]

[Sign-off (often first name only, no formal title)]
```

## Common TikTok outreach mistakes

- **Sounding too formal**: kills credibility on TikTok
- **Mentioning "deliverables" in first DM**: business-language too early; creators interpret as "they don't trust me"
- **Asking for video-script approval upfront**: creators value creative control; this is a no
- **Requesting exclusivity in first DM**: only for paid mid-tier+
- **Pretending to be a fan when you're not**: TikTok culture detects this instantly
- **Long brand-storytelling paragraph**: TikTok doesn't reward that

## TikTok-specific tactical recommendations

- **Reference the specific video; don't say "love your content"**: vague compliments get ignored
- **Use TikTok-native phrasing**: "POV:," "no but seriously," "✨" — only when on-brand
- **Send when creators are most likely active (lunch hours their time, late evening)**: TikTok creators check DMs at unconventional times
- **Don't expect immediate reply**: many TikTok creators batch-check DMs once a week
- **The TikTok Creator Marketplace** (for brands with TikTok For Business accounts) is a more reliable channel for mid-tier+

## TikTok Creator Marketplace path

For brands with TikTok For Business accounts, the Creator Marketplace offers:
- Discovery + filtering by audience demographic, niche, location
- In-platform messaging
- Contract templates
- Performance tracking
- Payment processing

The skill recommends Creator Marketplace as the channel of choice for:
- All paid partnerships above $500
- All macro outreach
- Campaigns requiring multiple creators with similar deliverables

DM is faster for ad-hoc one-off gifted outreach to nano/micro.
