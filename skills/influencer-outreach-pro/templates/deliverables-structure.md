# Deliverables Structure

The specific posts/content the brand expects from each partnership. Concrete deliverables convert; vague ones don't.

## Standard deliverables packages by tier + framework

### Nano + gifted

Typically no formal deliverables. Creator posts at their discretion. Expectation: ~50% post rate; ~25% post with full effort; ~25% don't post.

If buyer needs guarantees, move to hybrid framework.

### Nano + hybrid

- 1 Reel + 2 Stories with link (Instagram)
- 1 TikTok video + 1 caption mention (TikTok)
- Timing: within 14-21 days of receiving product
- Usage rights: organic only (brand cannot repost without permission)

### Micro + gifted (rare)

Same as nano gifted. Creator decides.

### Micro + hybrid

- 1 feed post + 3 Stories with link (Instagram standard)
- 1 dedicated TikTok video + Story share (TikTok)
- 1 in-grid post + dedicated Story sequence (Pinterest)
- Timing: within 14-30 days of receiving product
- Usage rights: organic + 30-day brand repost rights

### Micro + paid

- 1 feed post + 1 Reel + 3-5 Stories with link
- 1 dedicated TikTok video + 2 follow-up content pieces
- 1 dedicated YouTube integration (60-90 second mention within a longer video)
- Timing: within 14-21 days
- Usage rights: organic + 60-day brand repost rights + option to whitelist for paid ads

### Mid-tier + hybrid

- 1 dedicated feed post + 1 Reel + Story sequence
- 1 dedicated TikTok video + integration in adjacent content
- 1 dedicated YouTube video segment (90-180 seconds)
- Timing: within 21-45 days
- Usage rights: organic + 90-day brand rights + paid ad whitelist option

### Mid-tier + paid

- 2 feed posts + 2 Reels + extensive Story sequence
- Pinterest Idea Pin + standard Pins
- Dedicated YouTube video (full feature) or longer integration
- LinkedIn long-form post + carousel
- Timing: within 30-60 days
- Usage rights: full content rights for paid ad reuse + 30-90 day exclusivity in product category

### Macro + paid

Negotiated per campaign. Typical packages:
- 3+ pieces across platforms
- Dedicated YouTube video as anchor
- Cross-platform amplification (Reels + Shorts + Stories)
- Paid ad whitelisting standard
- 30-180 day exclusivity windows

## Deliverable specifications

For each deliverable, specify:

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| Format | Reel / Story / Feed post / TikTok / YouTube video / LinkedIn post / Pin |
| Quantity | How many of each |
| Content focus | Brand mention vs. dedicated product feature vs. integration |
| Length | Video duration (e.g., "90+ second YouTube integration") |
| Link placement | Bio link / swipe-up / linked in description / pinned comment |
| Hashtag inclusion | Required hashtags from brand |
| Brand mention | Brand handle tagged / brand name spoken |
| Posting window | Specific dates (start - end) |
| Brand approval | Whether brand reviews before posting (specify, but often "no — creator has full creative control") |
| Usage rights | Organic only / repost rights / paid ad whitelist / exclusive |
| Exclusivity | None / category exclusivity for X days |

## Sample deliverables paragraph for outreach

For a typical Micro Hybrid:

```
Deliverables we have in mind (open to your suggestions):
- 1 dedicated Reel showing the product in use (your creative latitude — happy to suggest angles)
- 3 Stories within 30 days of receiving the product, including a swipe-up link to our site
- Posts go up between June 15 and July 15
- Standard FTC disclosure (Instagram's Branded Content tool + #ad)
- Usage rights: organic posts stay yours; we'd love the option to repost to our feed with credit (no obligation on your end)
- No exclusivity required

Open to your input on any of these — you know what works for your audience better than we do.
```

The skill outputs deliverables as a clear, modular list rather than buried in prose.

## What to negotiate vs. what to set firmly

**Brand sets firmly:**
- Disclosure requirements (legally required)
- Timing window (campaign launch date)
- Brand mention / tag

**Open to negotiation:**
- Content format (creator may prefer Story over Reel)
- Specific deliverable counts
- Posting cadence
- Creative angle (creator suggestions usually improve)

**Creator usually sets:**
- Creative content / script (don't write the script for them)
- Posting time of day
- Caption voice + length
- Hashtag inclusion beyond required ones

The skill respects this division in the outreach draft. Brands that try to script the post or mandate the exact caption convert poorly — creators want creative control.

## Usage rights breakdown

The usage rights term is often the most negotiated:

| Right | Description | Typical cost premium |
|---|---|---|
| Organic only | Post lives on creator's feed, brand cannot repost | Baseline |
| Brand feed repost | Brand can repost to brand's own social feeds | +10-25% |
| Paid ad whitelist | Brand can run the creator's content as a paid ad | +50-200% |
| Exclusive paid ad | Time-bound exclusive use as paid ad | +75-300% |
| Perpetual rights | Brand owns the content forever | +200-500% |

The skill recommends modest usage rights for nano/micro (organic + repost) and full rights for mid-tier+ paid partnerships.

## Exclusivity windows

Exclusivity means the creator agrees not to work with competitors for a period.

| Tier | Typical exclusivity | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Nano | None | n/a |
| Micro | None or 7-14 days | +5-15% |
| Mid-tier | 14-30 days | +20-30% |
| Macro | 30-90 days standard | +40-100% |

Exclusivity is category-specific ("no other pour-over brands"), not whole-niche ("no other coffee brands"). Overbroad exclusivity reduces creator interest.

## Performance bonuses

Some paid partnerships include performance bonuses:

- "+ $X if the post earns N saves/shares" (engagement bonus)
- "+ X% of attributed sales above $Y" (conversion bonus)
- "+ $X if we whitelist the content for paid ads and it hits $Z spend"

The skill recommends bonuses only when:
- The brand has reliable tracking
- The creator has demonstrated past performance
- The base rate is already at the market range (bonus doesn't replace fair payment)

Tacking on "performance bonus" to lowball the base rate signals to creators that the brand isn't serious.

## Deliverables that almost never work

The skill flags these as anti-patterns:

- **Brand-written caption that the creator must use verbatim**: creators will refuse or push back
- **Brand approval of every post before publication**: signals distrust + slows process
- **"X posts per month" recurring deliverables on a 1-time payment**: creators feel exploited
- **Cross-platform deliverables paid as one platform**: each platform should be priced separately
- **"Make me a viral post"**: not contractually deliverable

## Reading the cache for repeat collaborations

When the buyer comes back to a creator who's done a prior partnership:

```json
"deliverables_history": [
  {
    "campaign_name": "spring_launch_2026",
    "date_agreed": "2026-04-15",
    "deliverables_promised": ["1 Reel", "3 Stories"],
    "deliverables_completed": ["1 Reel", "4 Stories"],
    "performance": {"reach": 18000, "engagement": 1450, "saves": 84},
    "creator_satisfaction": "high",
    "buyer_satisfaction": "high"
  }
]
```

The skill uses this history to:
- Calibrate new outreach (creator over-delivered last time → can ask for more this time)
- Reference past success in the new outreach ("hey, your last Reel for us crushed it — we'd love to do another round")
- Adjust payment expectations (creators expect raises for proven performers)

## Quality check before partnership starts

Before any contract is signed:

✓ Creator has demonstrated post engagement (not just follower count)
✓ Creator's recent content reflects current voice (not stale)
✓ Disclosure requirements are agreed upfront
✓ Deliverables and timing are explicit
✓ Usage rights and exclusivity are clear
✓ Payment terms (when / how / amount) are confirmed
✓ Brand has product ready to ship by the agreed timing
✓ Both parties have an exit clause for material non-performance

The skill embeds a brief deliverables-confirmation checklist in the outreach so the creator sees the terms upfront.

## When deliverables drift

If a creator completes 1 of 3 promised deliverables:

- First instance: skill recommends polite check-in ("hey, just confirming the remaining posts — let me know if anything came up")
- Second instance with same creator: skill flags `relationship_stage: strained` + recommends not re-collaborating without resolution
- Pattern across multiple campaigns: flag and require buyer review before any future outreach

The skill maintains creator deliverables reputation across campaigns — a creator who reliably under-delivers gets flagged, regardless of follower count.
