# Platform: X / Twitter

X (Twitter) creator outreach is different from visual platforms. Creators here are often opinion leaders, journalists, analysts, or community voices rather than traditional product-focused influencers. Outreach should respect that distinction.

## Channel options

- **X DM** (if open / following each other): primary
- **Email** (if listed in bio): preferred for serious business outreach
- **Reply to a post** (warm reply only, never as cold pitch)
- **Quote tweet** (never for outreach)

Note: X DMs from non-followers go to "Message requests" — often unchecked for months.

## Length

X creators prefer brevity. Short messages get read; long messages get scrolled past.

| Tier | DM length |
|---|---|
| Nano | 50-80 words |
| Micro | 70-100 words |
| Mid-tier | 80-130 words |
| Macro | use email |

## Tone

- **Nano**: peer-to-peer, conversational, often a callback to something they tweeted
- **Micro**: warm + direct; X culture values brevity
- **Mid-tier**: professional + still concise; X readers parse fast
- **Macro**: business email, formal

## X outreach is NOT product-seeding-first

X creators rarely make traditional "I tried this product" content. The platform's primary content forms are:
- Long-form threads (insights, breakdowns, analysis)
- Quote tweets (commentary)
- Original posts (opinions, observations)
- Replies + community engagement

Brand partnerships on X work better when:
- Brand sponsors a thread or analysis (paid spot in long-form content)
- Brand becomes a recurring topic in a creator's niche-relevant posts
- Brand provides data/expertise the creator threads on

Don't pitch X creators on "make a video about our product" — wrong platform. Pitch on:
- Sponsored thread about [industry topic the brand is expert in]
- Long-form Twitter post mentioning brand as case study
- Live event coverage (e.g., trade show, launch)

## Personalization on X

Reference specific tweets, threads, or quote-tweet patterns:

> "Hi Alex,
>
> Your thread last week on independent coffee brands hit something we've been wrestling with — specifically your point about supply chain transparency being more important than certifications. We're a small ceramic brand thinking about exactly that question."

Specific thread reference + specific point + brand context.

## Required disclosures on X

- **Gifted only**: #gift or #ad — X enforces less aggressively than Instagram but FTC still requires
- **Hybrid / Paid**: #ad / #sponsored in the tweet or thread
- **Affiliate**: #affiliate

X creators sometimes hide disclosures in thread-end (last tweet of a long thread). The skill flags this and recommends asking the creator to disclose in the lead tweet, not thread-end.

## Outreach structure (X-specific)

```
Hi [First name],

[Specific reference to a recent thread/tweet — 1-2 sentences]

[Brand intro compressed — 1 sentence]

[Why this would fit their X content (NOT general product seeding) — 1-2 sentences]

[Offer — what specific type of content + structure — 1-2 sentences]

[Soft ask — "open to chatting more?" — 1 short sentence]

[Sign-off + first name]
```

## Common X outreach mistakes

- **Treating X creators like Instagram creators**: different content forms = different pitches
- **Asking for "a tweet about our product"**: too generic; X creators want creative latitude
- **Sending product samples with no context**: wasted unless the creator's content format actually uses products
- **Cold-DM'ing journalists/analysts**: many block; better to engage via reply first then escalate to email
- **Using emoji excessively**: X culture varies; some accounts are emoji-rich, others not — match the creator's style
- **Pitching during a news cycle that overshadows everything**: nobody cares about your brand DM during breaking news

## X-specific tactical recommendations

- **Read 5-10 recent tweets before drafting**: get the voice right
- **Mention the creator's audience size only if you have a specific reason**: many X creators have ironic distance from their follower count
- **Avoid "thought leader" framing**: cringe on X
- **Don't pitch immediately after they post something controversial**: bad timing
- **Use the platform's "Twitter Blue paid checkmark" awareness**: some creators are sensitive about the verification politics

## When NOT to use X for outreach

If the brand has no real X presence or content angle that fits X, skip the platform. X creators detect "we're trying to expand to X because the others got expensive" framing and ignore.

The skill flags this when the buyer's brand has no X account or posts < 1×/month — recommends focusing on platforms where the brand naturally lives.
