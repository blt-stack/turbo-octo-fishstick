# Platform: Instagram

The most common platform for brand-creator outreach in e-commerce. DM-driven; many creators check DMs daily but message-request inbox gets flooded.

## Channel options

- **DM (direct message)**: most common; lands in primary inbox if you're followed back, otherwise in message requests
- **Email** (if visible in bio or via creator marketplace): most professional; preferred for mid-tier + macro
- **Comment** (very rarely): inappropriate for outreach; only use for warm replies

The skill defaults to DM for nano/micro and email for mid-tier/macro when email is known.

## Message request inbox reality

Instagram filters DMs from non-followers into the message requests folder. Most creators check this 1-3× per week, not daily. Open rate is significantly lower than primary inbox.

The skill flags this in the report — buyer should expect 30-60% no-reply rate even on well-crafted outreach simply because the message never got seen.

To get into primary inbox:
- Follow the creator first (low-friction)
- Engage with 1-2 of their recent posts before DM'ing (commenting, story replies)
- Be on their follow list (rare for cold outreach but possible if they followed you back)

## Length

| Tier | DM length |
|---|---|
| Nano | 80-120 words |
| Micro | 100-150 words |
| Mid-tier | 120-180 words (typically transition to email) |
| Macro | use email |

Long DMs get skimmed or ignored. Front-load the value.

## Tone

- **Nano**: peer-to-peer warm ("hey [first name]!"; lowercase ok; emoji ok if on-brand)
- **Micro**: friendly professional ("Hi [first name]"; some emoji; full punctuation)
- **Mid-tier**: professional ("Hi [first name]" or "Hi [handle]"; minimal emoji; full punctuation)
- **Macro**: formal, often manager-targeted ("Hi [creator name] team" if managed; otherwise "Hi [name]")

## Personalization is non-optional

Instagram outreach without specific reference to recent content reads as a bot/template. The skill always weaves in:

- A specific recent post reference (caption phrase, content topic, visual choice)
- Why the brand noticed them specifically (not generic "love your content")
- A genuine compliment that's specific, not flattery

Example bad opener (template detected):
> "Hi @creator! Love your aesthetic and authentic content. We'd love to partner!"

Example good opener (personalized):
> "Hi Maya — your post about brewing pour-over on the camping trip last week made me actually try it myself this weekend. The way you explained the bloom step finally clicked for me."

The skill checks that the opener contains at least one specific reference (a topic, a caption phrase, or a visual element from the pasted post context) before outputting.

## Required disclosures on Instagram

- **Gifted only**: #gift or "gifted by [brand]" — FTC requires "material connection" disclosure
- **Hybrid / Paid**: "Paid partnership with [brand]" tag at top of post (uses Instagram's built-in branded content tool), plus #ad or #sponsored
- **Affiliate**: #affiliate disclosure + Instagram's branded content tool if a partnership is also involved

The skill includes the disclosure expectation in the outreach so the creator knows what's required upfront.

## Outreach structure (Instagram-specific)

```
[Specific personalized opener — 1-2 sentences referencing recent post]

[Brand intro — 1 sentence]

[Product fit — 1-2 sentences on why this product fits THEIR content specifically]

[Offer — 1-2 sentences on what's being offered (gifted/paid/structure)]

[Clear ask + soft CTA — 1 sentence]

[Sign-off + name]
```

## Common Instagram outreach mistakes

- **Sending the same DM to 50 creators**: Instagram flags spam patterns; account gets restricted
- **Using "DM us" CTA** (when you're the brand DM'ing them): they'd reply in the same thread — say "let me know!"
- **Asking for "honest review"**: legally fraught (FTC) plus signals unsophisticated outreach
- **Including a link in the first DM**: lands in spam filter for non-followers
- **All caps anywhere**
- **Excessive emoji**
- **"Hey gorgeous / babe / love"**: cringe; reads as fake-warm

## Instagram-specific tactical recommendations

- **Send DMs Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM creator's time zone**: highest open rate window
- **Don't send late evening or weekends**: looks unprofessional + lower open rate
- **Wait 24 hours minimum after engaging with their content before DM'ing**: looks less spam-like
- **Send no more than 30-50 outreach DMs per day per account**: above this, Instagram throttles
- **Track which DMs got opened (read receipt)**: useful signal for follow-up timing
