# Shopify PDP Format

Long-form, conversion-focused. Shopify has no hard character limits the way marketplaces do, but pages that work tend to share a structure.

## Structure

```
1. TITLE (60-70 chars ideal for SEO snippet)
   - Format: [Product] — [Key Differentiator] | [Brand]
   - Lead with the product noun, not the brand

2. HERO HOOK (1-2 sentences, ~30 words)
   - Names who it's for and what it does
   - Avoid feature-listing here — sell the outcome

3. VALUE PROPOSITION BLOCK (3-5 bullets)
   - Each bullet = one benefit, supported by one feature
   - Format: "**[Outcome]** — [feature that delivers it]"
   - Keep bullets to 12-18 words

4. STORY / DETAIL SECTION (150-300 words)
   - Narrative prose, not bullets
   - Materials, origin, who makes it, why it exists
   - This is where brand voice does the most work

5. SPECS / WHAT'S INCLUDED (bullets)
   - Dimensions, materials, weight, what's in the box, care instructions
   - Functional and scannable, voice can dial down here

6. FAQ (3-5 questions)
   - Real objections, answered in the brand voice
   - Don't fabricate — base on common questions for the category

7. META DESCRIPTION (150-160 chars, hard limit)
   - For Google SERP snippet
   - Includes primary keyword and one CTA
```

## Voice notes

- Shopify gives you room to breathe. Use it. Marketplaces strip voice — Shopify is where you build the brand.
- Subheadings can be playful ("Why we made this", "Care, basically") — they help scanability without sacrificing voice.
- Hyperlinks are allowed and useful (link to size guide, related collections, About page).
- Don't repeat the product title in the hero hook — assume the user is already on the page and the title is right above.

## Pitfalls

- **Generic openers** ("Introducing the..." / "Discover...") signal AI-generated copy. Avoid.
- **All-feature, no-benefit bullets.** "Made of 100% organic cotton" is a feature. "Sleeps like nothing's there because it's woven from 100% organic cotton" is a benefit + feature.
- **Walls of text.** Use white space, subheadings, and bullets to break up the story section.
- **Calling out price points.** Shopify lists the price separately. Don't write "only $49" in the body.
