## Notes on this exemplar

- Character counts are shown inline so the buyer can verify nothing's over limit.
- Each platform shows two variants except where the format guide says one is enough (eBay defaults to one).
- Sections that depend on buyer config (`{{brand_name}}`, shop-wide shipping info) use placeholders the renderer fills in.
- The story section and FAQ for Shopify are doing the most voice work — Amazon and Walmart strip voice harder.
- The TikTok Shop variant feels noticeably different in voice register — that's correct. Each platform has its own audience.
