# Dispute Drafts — Platform-Removal Requests

When a review is flagged as fake/competitor/abusive in a way that violates platform policy, the skill drafts a removal-request submission to the platform. Each platform has different criteria for what's removable, different submission paths, and different success rates.

The dispute draft is what the buyer copy-pastes into the platform's review-removal workflow.

## When to file a dispute

| Sentiment subtype | File dispute? |
|---|---|
| Constructive 1★ | Never — legitimate negative reviews are not removable |
| Abusive (profanity, personal attack) | Yes — most platforms remove abuse |
| Fake / competitor | Yes — most platforms remove if evidence is provided |
| Sycophantic | No — paid positive reviews are not the buyer's problem to dispute |
| Off-topic / wrong product | Yes on Amazon and Etsy; usually possible elsewhere |
| Reviewer threatens / extorts ("Refund or I'll leave a 1★") | Yes — explicit extortion is removable on most platforms |

The dispute draft is **optional**. The buyer decides whether to submit. Some buyers prefer to leave the review and reply publicly; others file aggressively. The skill provides the draft so the option is on the table.

## Per-platform dispute paths

### Shopify review apps (Judge.me, Loox, Yotpo, Stamped, Reviews.io)

Each app has an in-dashboard "report review" or "hide review" function. Most allow the merchant to hide a review from the public store without removing it from analytics. Dispute language matters less here because the merchant has more direct control.

**Dispute draft (Judge.me / Loox / Yotpo internal report):**

```
Reason for report: [Off-topic / Inappropriate language / Spam / Not a verified customer / Other policy violation]

Details:
This review was posted by [Reviewer name], but our customer records show no order under this name or email. The review references [specific detail], which does not match any product we sell / matches a product we discontinued before the review date. We believe this is [fake / competitor / spam] and request removal under [platform]'s authenticity policy.

Evidence:
- No customer record for this reviewer
- Reviewer's account was created [date], with no other purchases
- [Any additional evidence]

Thank you.
```

### Amazon Seller Reviews

Amazon allows removal requests via the "Report abuse" function on each review and via Seller Support cases for systemic issues. Amazon will remove reviews that:
- Contain profanity, hate speech, or threats
- Are off-topic (e.g., review about shipping rather than product)
- Were posted by the brand or competitors
- Contain promotional content for other products
- Reference specific sellers (Amazon's review policy is product-centric, not seller-centric)

**Dispute draft (Amazon "Report abuse" submission):**

```
This review violates Amazon's Customer Reviews Guidelines for the following reason: [pick the most fitting]

[ ] Inappropriate content (profanity, abusive language, threats)
[ ] Off-topic (review focuses on shipping, seller, or unrelated matter, not the product)
[ ] Suspected fake review (reviewer pattern indicates incentivized or competitor review)
[ ] Promotes a competitor product
[ ] Contains personal information

Details:
[Specific reason in 2-3 sentences. Reference Amazon's review guidelines language directly. Cite specific phrases or evidence from the review.]

We have reviewed this customer's order history and [find no record of purchase / find suspicious patterns]. The review's content [specific issue]. We respectfully request review removal per Amazon's policy.

Submitted by: [Brand name]
Date: [date]
```

Notes:
- Amazon does NOT remove negative-but-honest reviews even with extensive evidence
- Amazon's process is slow (10-30 days)
- Filing more than 5 disputes a month gets reduced response priority — be selective

### Etsy

Etsy allows reviewers to leave reviews only on verified purchases, which means most fake/competitor reviews are pre-blocked. The dispute path is via Etsy Seller Support, and Etsy will remove reviews that:
- Contain profanity, threats, or hate speech
- Are about shipping issues (Etsy considers shipping reviews off-topic and routinely removes them)
- Are about communication / personal interactions outside the product
- Threaten extortion ("Refund or 1 star")

**Dispute draft (Etsy support case):**

```
Subject: Request to remove review #[review-id] under Etsy's review policy

Hello,

I'm writing to request review of a recent review on my shop [shop name] for [item name]. The review #[review-id] from [reviewer username] contains [policy violation: inappropriate language / off-topic content / extortion attempt / other].

Specifically, the review states: "[quote the policy-violating portion]"

Under Etsy's review policy, reviews must [cite specific policy provision]. This review violates that policy because [specific reason in 1-2 sentences].

I have responded professionally to the review and addressed the customer's underlying concern, but I believe the review itself does not meet Etsy's standards for buyer reviews.

Thank you for your time.
[Seller name]
[Shop name]
```

### Google Business Profile

Google's review removal policy is narrow. Google will remove reviews that:
- Contain spam / fake content (multiple reviews from the same source, off-topic)
- Are off-topic
- Are restricted content (illegal, profanity, sexually explicit)
- Are personal attacks
- Are conflicts of interest (competitor reviews, employee reviews)

Google explicitly does NOT remove negative-but-honest reviews. Submitting a low-merit request can result in losing future submission priority.

**Dispute draft (Google Maps flag for removal, via the three-dot menu on the review):**

```
Reason: [Spam / Off-topic / Personal attack / Conflict of interest / Restricted content]

This review violates Google's review policy because [specific reason]:

- [Evidence point 1: e.g., "Reviewer has left reviews for our top 3 competitors with nearly identical language within a 48-hour window"]
- [Evidence point 2: e.g., "Reviewer's profile shows no other activity since the account was created on [date]"]
- [Evidence point 3]

The review is [fake / off-topic / a personal attack], not an honest customer experience. Requesting removal under Google's [Spam and fake content / Off-topic / Restricted content] policy.
```

### Trustpilot

Trustpilot has a flagging system + a formal "Report this review" path. Trustpilot will remove reviews that:
- Are not based on a real experience (the burden of proof is on the brand)
- Contain harmful or illegal content
- Reveal personally identifiable information
- Are about a different company
- Promote other businesses
- Violate intellectual property rights

Trustpilot's process is more rigorous than Google's. Provide evidence.

**Dispute draft (Trustpilot "Report" with evidence package):**

```
Reason for report: [Pick from Trustpilot's enumerated list — most often "Not based on a genuine experience" or "Violates Trustpilot's guidelines"]

Specific guideline violated: [Quote the specific Trustpilot guideline]

Why this review violates the policy:

1. **No record of customer relationship.** We have no record of [Reviewer name / email / order detail] in our customer database, which covers all orders since [date].

2. **Specific evidence of inauthenticity:**
   - [Detail 1 — e.g., "Reviewer mentions product [X], but we have never sold product X"]
   - [Detail 2 — e.g., "Review was posted on [date], but our domain was inactive for 3 days due to a server migration during that window"]
   - [Detail 3]

3. **Pattern evidence (if applicable):**
   - [E.g., "Three other 1-star reviews using nearly identical language were posted within a 72-hour window from accounts with no other Trustpilot activity"]

We've responded to the review publicly to maintain transparency, but believe Trustpilot's authenticity guidelines support removal. Happy to provide additional documentation.

Thank you,
[Brand name representative]
[Email]
```

Trustpilot occasionally requests proof of identity/customer status to verify a reviewer. If the reviewer can't provide proof, Trustpilot removes the review. This is the most effective dispute mechanism on the platform.

### Facebook Page Reviews / Recommendations

Facebook's review system is now "Recommendations," with each rated as "Yes/No" rather than star-based. Removal happens via the page's review settings + flag-as-spam. Facebook will remove:
- Spam (clear marketing for unrelated products)
- Hate speech / threats
- Off-topic (a recommendation that's clearly about a different page)
- Coordinated inauthentic behavior

**Dispute draft (Facebook recommendation flag):**

```
Reason for flag: [Spam / Hate speech / Doesn't follow Community Standards / Coordinated inauthentic behavior]

Specific concern: This recommendation [doesn't reflect a genuine customer experience / contains spam / is part of a coordinated negative campaign].

Evidence:
- [Specific evidence relevant to the flag reason]

Requesting removal under Facebook's Community Standards.
```

Facebook's review system is the most permissive about removal because the page owner can effectively turn off Recommendations entirely. The dispute is less critical if the page owner is willing to disable the feature.

## What NEVER goes in a dispute draft

- Calling the reviewer a liar by name
- Asking the platform to "punish" the reviewer
- Threats of legal action against the platform
- Emotional language ("This is destroying my business")
- Multiple disputes filed in rapid succession (looks coordinated; reduces credibility)
- Disputes against legitimate negative reviews (every platform sees this and discounts future legitimate disputes)
- Personal information about the reviewer (their address, phone number) — privacy violations work against the brand

## Filing strategy

For most brands, the right cadence is:
- File disputes only on the clearest violations
- Wait 7-14 days for response before re-filing
- Keep a record of which platforms approved / denied which disputes (this helps tune future submissions)
- Pair every dispute with a posted public reply — the public reply protects the brand if the dispute fails

The skill outputs the dispute draft alongside the public reply so the buyer has both options regardless of which path they choose.
