# Platform: Google Business Profile

Google reviews are the highest-SEO-weight reviews of any platform. Replies are indexed by Google search, surface in the "People also ask" knowledge panel, and influence local pack rankings. Every reply is permanently part of the brand's SEO footprint.

## Reply visibility

- **Public:** Yes — appears directly under the review in Google Maps / Google Search
- **Indexed:** Strongly — Google indexes its own reviews + replies extensively
- **Edit/delete:** Yes — replies can be edited or deleted at any time
- **Reply quota:** One reply per review

## SEO consideration

Because Google indexes replies, the skill applies an SEO-aware overlay:

- **Naturally include the product name and one or two key features** in the reply (without keyword stuffing)
- **Naturally include the brand name** once (Google rewards this)
- **Don't over-optimize** — Google's algorithm penalizes obviously keyword-stuffed replies

Example of SEO-aware 5★ reply:

> Hi Priya,
>
> Thank you for the kind words about the River Stone Pour-Over — hearing that the balance "feels like it was made by a person" is exactly what we hope for at Stone & Glass. Pieces like this one were thrown in our Brooklyn studio.
>
> — Jamie

The reply naturally references the product name, brand name, and key feature without forcing it. Google indexes this. A future searcher looking for "Stone & Glass pour-over" sees this reply in their search results.

## Character limit

Google's limit is 4000 characters. Target: **60-140 words for recovery + clarify, 35-70 for amplify**. Mobile readability is the real constraint, not Google's hard limit.

## Tone register

**Slightly more polished than Shopify, slightly less formal than Amazon.** Google reviews surface to a broader audience (anyone searching for the brand), so the reply should be professional but warm.

## Reply structure (Google-specific)

For 1-2★ recovery:

```
Hi [First name],

We're sorry [specific issue] — that's not the experience we want for anyone shopping with [brand name].

[Concrete fix in one sentence.]

Please email us at hello@brand.com so we can follow up directly.

— [First name], [Brand name]
```

For 3★ clarify:

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for the honest review and especially for [specific positive about brand/product].

We'd love to know more about [the friction] — was it [option A] or [option B]? Even a quick reply helps us improve.

— [First name], [Brand name]
```

For 4-5★ amplify:

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for the kind review of [product name]. Hearing that [specific praise] is exactly what we hope for at [brand name].

— [First name]
```

## Google-specific allowances

- **Email addresses** can be included — Google considers them legitimate channel handoff
- **Website URLs** can be included (use sparingly — once is fine)
- **Specific employee names** allowed
- **Brand name and product name** mention is rewarded by Google's indexing

## Google-specific restrictions

Google's review policy prohibits:
- Restricted content (sexually explicit, illegal, hate speech)
- Off-topic content
- Spam
- Personal information (don't post the reviewer's email, phone, etc.)
- Conflicts of interest (don't post replies pretending to be a different customer)
- Promotional content unrelated to the customer's experience

Replies don't get scrutinized as heavily as reviews, but they're still subject to these rules.

## Google removal disputes

Google removes reviews that violate the above policies. The removal-request workflow is via the 3-dot menu on each review (flag for removal). See `dispute-drafts.md` for the dispute draft template.

Google's response rate on removal disputes is **moderate**. Clearly off-topic and clearly spammy reviews get removed within days. Borderline cases (vague negative reviews from unknown reviewers) usually don't.

## Local SEO considerations

For brands with a physical location (or that serve a local geography), Google reviews carry double weight: they influence Google Maps ranking AND knowledge panel content. The skill flags Google reviews for "high SEO priority handling" in the bulk report.

For pure-online brands, Google reviews still matter (knowledge panel, search results) but less than for local businesses.

## Posting workflow

1. Buyer goes to Google Business Profile dashboard
2. Reviews tab → find the review
3. Click "Reply"
4. Paste the drafted reply
5. Post

Replies appear immediately. Posting cadence isn't usually a constraint — Google doesn't rate-limit reply posting.

## Common Google review patterns

### "I never bought anything from this business" review

If the reviewer claims they never purchased from the brand and the brand has no record of them, dispute as off-topic. See `dispute-drafts.md`.

### "Wrong location" review

Sometimes Google reviews get posted to the wrong business by accident. Reply politely noting the apparent mistake, and file a dispute as off-topic.

```
Hi [Name],

It looks like this review may be intended for a different business — we don't have a record of [the experience described]. If we can help, please email hello@brand.com.

— [Name]
```

### "Slow response from customer service" review

For brands with a small team, this is common. Recovery reply that owns the response time + commits to a follow-up.

```
Hi [Name],

A three-day wait isn't the response time we aim for, and I'm sorry. I just emailed you personally to follow up. We're working on response time and your review is a useful nudge.

— [Founder name], founder, [Brand]
```

## Founder-signed Google replies

For high-stakes Google reviews (1★, high-visibility, public complaint risk), recommend founder-signed reply. Google reviews with founder responses convert curious-search visitors significantly better than CS-signed responses.
