# Platform: Trustpilot

Trustpilot is the highest-stakes review platform for DTC brands. Reviews are aggressively indexed by Google, the TrustScore badge appears in Google rich snippets for many brand-name searches, and a low TrustScore can suppress conversion rates by 20-40%. Every reply on Trustpilot is permanent SEO and trust signal.

## Reply visibility

- **Public:** Yes — appears under the review on the brand's Trustpilot profile
- **Indexed:** Strongly — Trustpilot reviews show in Google rich results, snippets, and brand searches
- **Edit/delete:** Yes — replies can be edited
- **Reply quota:** One reply per review

## Trustpilot policy framework

Trustpilot has a relatively strict moderation policy. Brands can flag reviews for:
- Not based on a genuine customer experience
- Harmful or illegal content
- Reveals personally identifiable information
- About a different company
- Promotes other businesses
- Violates intellectual property

Trustpilot's response to disputes is **better than Google's** — they often require the reviewer to provide proof of customer relationship. If the reviewer can't, the review is removed.

## Character limit

10000 characters. Practically: target **80-180 words for recovery + clarify, 40-80 for amplify**. Trustpilot's display truncates long replies; readability matters more than length.

## Tone register

**Professional, polished, brand-aware.** Trustpilot's audience is comparing the brand to competitors. The reply tone should signal "this is a brand that takes customer experience seriously."

A bit more formal than Shopify, a bit less formal than Amazon.

## Reply structure (Trustpilot-specific)

For 1-2★ recovery:

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for taking the time to share this — and we're truly sorry [specific issue].

[Concrete fix in one sentence. On Trustpilot, it's appropriate to be more explicit about the fix since the audience is comparison-shopping.]

We've reached out to you directly at [email reference — don't post the actual email] to resolve this. If you haven't received our message, please check your spam folder or reply directly to this review.

Thank you for the honest feedback. It helps us improve.

— [First name], [Brand name]
```

For 3★ clarify:

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for the balanced review — and especially for [specific positive].

We'd love to learn more about [the specific friction]. If you have a minute, please reply here or email us at hello@brand.com so we can [follow-up action].

— [First name], [Brand name]
```

For 4-5★ amplify:

```
Hi [First name],

Thank you for the kind review. Hearing that [specific praise] means a lot to our team at [brand name].

We hope to continue earning your trust.

— [First name]
```

## Trustpilot-specific allowances

- **Email addresses** allowed (Trustpilot permits direct email handoff)
- **Website URLs** allowed (use sparingly — once is enough)
- **Brand language** that's slightly more polished than other platforms

## Trustpilot-specific cautions

- **Public refund offers**: avoid. Trustpilot replies should reference that resolution is happening privately, not the specifics. Specifics belong in the private email.
- **Arguing with the reviewer**: never. Trustpilot's audience punishes brands that argue.
- **Bulk-pasting the same reply**: Trustpilot flags this. Each reply must be unique-ish.
- **Posting customer's PII**: don't reference order numbers, full names, addresses, or personal contact info in the reply.

## Founder-signed Trustpilot replies

Trustpilot is the platform where founder-signed replies have the highest measured conversion impact. Recommend founder signing for:
- All 1★ reviews
- Any 2★ from a customer with order count > 1 (repeat customer)
- Any review with measurable public-complaint risk (mentions of "I'll never shop here again," "telling everyone")

The reply should say "I'm [Founder name], the founder of [brand]" at some point. This signals authenticity.

## Dispute strategy on Trustpilot

Trustpilot's dispute success rate is highest when:
- The brand can prove no customer relationship exists
- The review contains misleading information (wrong product, wrong company)
- The review uses language clearly inconsistent with a real customer
- Multiple suspect reviews cluster together suspiciously

The skill's fake/competitor detection has its highest signal on Trustpilot — many brands receive coordinated negative review campaigns from competitors or disgruntled ex-employees. See `dispute-drafts.md` for the Trustpilot-specific dispute template.

## TrustScore management

Each new 1★ pulls the TrustScore down measurably. Each new 5★ pushes it up. The skill's bulk mode is especially valuable for brands recovering from a bad cluster — replying to 20 reviews in one batch with high-quality, varied replies signals to Trustpilot's algorithm and to future readers that the brand is engaged.

## Common Trustpilot patterns

### "I emailed customer service three times and nobody responded" (CS-failure recovery)

```
Hi [Name],

Three unanswered emails isn't acceptable, and I'm sorry it took a Trustpilot review for us to surface this. I'm Jamie, the founder — I've found your emails in our system and am personally responding now.

[Concrete fix in one sentence.]

You deserved a better experience the first time, and we'll do better.

— Jamie, founder, [Brand]
```

### "Product was nothing like advertised" (expectation mismatch)

```
Hi [Name],

We're sorry [product] didn't match your expectations from the listing. That mismatch is on us, not on you. We've issued a full refund and you don't need to return the item.

If you'd consider letting us know what specifically felt different from what we showed, that detail would help us update our listings.

— [Name], [Brand]
```

### "Worth every penny — best [category] I've ever owned" (amplify)

```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for the kind review. Hearing that [product] earned that recommendation from someone who's tried others in the category means a lot. We hope it serves you for years.

— [Name]
```

## Posting workflow

1. Buyer goes to Trustpilot Business → Reviews
2. Finds the review
3. Clicks "Reply"
4. Pastes the drafted reply
5. Posts

Trustpilot allows reply editing post-submission, so minor edits are recoverable. Major redo's aren't — the reviewer sees the edit timestamp.
