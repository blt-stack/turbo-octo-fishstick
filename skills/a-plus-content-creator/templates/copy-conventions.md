# A+ Content Copy Conventions

Amazon enforces strict content policies on A+ Content. Violations lead to module rejection on submission or content suppression after the fact. This file is the authoritative reference for cross-module copy rules.

## The mandatory compliance checklist

Every piece of A+ Content copy generated by this skill is checked against the following before it's written into the brief. Anything that fails is rewritten or flagged for the buyer.

### Prohibited claims (absolute — always)
- **No medical or health claims unless category-allowed:** "cures", "treats", "prevents", "FDA approved", "doctor recommended" — most categories prohibit these. Supplements have a separate set of allowed claims defined by FDA and reflected in Amazon's category guidelines.
- **No financial claims:** "Guaranteed returns", "Build wealth", "Pay off your mortgage" — financial products have their own rules.
- **No claims of being "best", "#1", "top-rated", "world's best"** — superlatives without verifiable substantiation get flagged.
- **No exaggerated outcome claims:** "Lose 30 pounds in 30 days", "Quadruple your productivity overnight" — Amazon's content review increasingly catches these via auto-classification.
- **No "miracle", "magic", "secret", "breakthrough"** — these trigger compliance review in nearly every category.

### Prohibited references
- **No competitor brand names.** Naming "Apple", "Tesla", "Whole Foods", or any other named brand is a TOS violation. Use "other brands", "the leading [category]", or "you may have used before".
- **No competitor product comparisons.** The Comparison Chart module can only compare against the seller's own catalog. Inter-brand comparisons violate TOS.
- **No mentions of other marketplaces.** "Also available on Etsy", "Find us on Shopify" — all banned.
- **No mentions of competitors' Amazon listings.** "Compare to ASIN B0XXXX" — banned.

### Prohibited promotional language
- **No price mentions.** "$49", "Just $19.99", "Now only" — Amazon controls all price display.
- **No promotional language.** "Sale", "Save 20%", "Limited time", "While supplies last", "Today only" — banned.
- **No urgency manufacturing.** "Order now before stock runs out", "Last chance" — Amazon's auto-review flags these.
- **No discount language.** "Free", "Bonus", "Gift with purchase" — Amazon controls promotions.

### Prohibited CTAs
- **No external website CTAs.** "Visit our website", "Sign up at...", "Find us at..." — TOS violation.
- **No email CTAs.** "Email us at...", "Contact us at...".
- **No social media CTAs.** "Follow us on Instagram", "Like our Facebook page".
- **No newsletter CTAs.** "Subscribe to our newsletter".
- **Generally allowed:** "See our [brand] storefront" with link rendered by Amazon. The skill leaves CTA language out by default.

### Prohibited content
- **No reviews / testimonials with attribution.** "Jane S. said..." is banned. Generic statements about customer experiences are allowed ("Most customers tell us..." is borderline; safer to avoid).
- **No trademark symbols (™, ®, ©) for marks you don't own.** Add only for marks the seller has registered.
- **No copyright violations** in text or imagery.
- **No prohibited substances** (drug names, controlled substances) outside category permission.

## Voice + tone within compliance constraints

Compliance defines the floor — what you can't say. The brand voice (loaded from `config.brand_voice`) defines the ceiling — how the copy sounds within those constraints.

The skill's job is to write copy that's:
- Compliant with every rule above
- True to the buyer's brand voice
- Specific (concrete > vague)
- Benefit-led (outcomes > features when possible)

### Cross-module tone consistency

Every module on a page should sound like the same brand. Avoid:
- Heroic-sounding hero copy followed by clinical bullet copy
- Brand-story copy in casual voice followed by FAQ copy in formal voice

Pick one voice register from the brand-voice profile and hold it across all modules.

## Character limits — quick reference

Limits per module type are documented in `module-library.md`. Cross-cutting rules:

- **Always show character counts** in the brief (`X/Y` format) so the buyer can verify nothing's over
- **Build to 80-90% of the limit, not 100%** — leaves room for last-minute edits
- **Never exceed the hard cap** — Amazon will truncate, often mid-word

## Keyword integration rules (when keyword cache is present)

When the Listing SEO Optimizer (skill #2) has written keywords to the shared cache, this skill weaves them in. Constraints:

- **Module headlines**: 1-2 keywords max, naturally placed (front-loaded if the keyword is the primary head term)
- **Module body copy**: keywords appear at the rate of ~1 per 100 characters of body — never stuffed
- **Image alt text**: 1 keyword per image when relevant, written naturally
- **Comparison Chart cells**: keywords appear in the row labels and headline, not in individual cells
- **FAQ questions**: a question can be phrased to include a keyword ("How does this [keyword] compare to [variant]?") but never forced

Across the whole A+ page, target ~80% keyword coverage of the top 5 cache keywords (most should appear at least once). Going for 100% coverage usually over-stuffs.

## Tone register guide

The brand voice profile typically specifies tone adjectives. Here's how to render each in A+ Content copy:

### Warm / approachable
- Conversational sentence rhythm
- "You" framing
- One sensory or scene detail per paragraph
- Avoid: institutional language, passive voice

### Direct / technical
- Short sentences
- Spec-led headlines
- Lower adjective count
- Avoid: hedging language ("might", "could", "perhaps")

### Playful / cheeky
- Punchy headlines
- A bit of personality in body copy (one wry observation per module max — A+ Content isn't social media)
- Avoid: jokes that don't land in writing, slang that dates fast

### Calm / reverent
- Longer paragraphs, fewer sentences
- Sensory imagery without overload
- Lowercase or sentence-case headings when possible
- Avoid: superlatives, exclamation points

### Bold / confident
- Strong claims (within compliance — "built for X", not "best for X")
- Active voice always
- Avoid: weak verbs (be-verbs, "is", "are" in headlines when stronger verbs exist)

The smart-pick algorithm doesn't change based on tone, but the *writing* of each module's copy does.

## When the buyer's content goes against compliance

Sometimes the buyer wants something the skill must refuse. Examples:
- "Add 'doctor recommended' to the headline" — refuse unless verifiable
- "Mention that we're better than [competitor]" — refuse, suggest a category-framed comparison
- "Add 'limited time only' to the hero" — refuse, suggest a non-urgency framing

The skill should:
1. Explain the rule that would be broken (specifically — "Amazon TOS prohibits naming competitor brands in A+ Content")
2. Offer a compliant alternative
3. Note that proceeding with the violation could get the listing suppressed or the seller flagged

If the buyer overrides ("just write it anyway"), draft it with a clearly-flagged WARNING in the brief so the buyer knows what they're choosing.

## The compliance check (final step before brief output)

Every brief includes a compliance check block. The skill self-audits and reports:

```
COMPLIANCE CHECK
- No prohibited claims: ✓ (or flag specific violations)
- No external URLs: ✓
- No competitor names: ✓
- No pricing language: ✓
- No prohibited CTAs: ✓
- All character limits respected: ✓
- All images within size + dimension limits: ✓
- Trademark symbols accurate: ✓
- Reviews / testimonials: none attributed (or "flagged: testimonial with name needs to be genericized")
```

Any ✗ should be flagged inline with a recommended fix. Don't ship a brief with unflagged compliance issues — the buyer trusts the skill to catch what they'd otherwise have to manually verify against Amazon's policy doc.
