paper, with a brew guide written for beginners. If it breaks from normal use, we replace

## Notes on this exemplar

- **Module rationale is transparent** — buyer sees why each module was picked and which were skipped
- **Character counts shown inline** for every copy field (e.g., "Headline (134/150)")
- **Generated images are embedded in the .docx** so the buyer reviews them in context
- **The .html preview lets the buyer "see the page"** before pasting to Amazon
- **Compliance check is a clear pass/fail list** — buyer doesn't have to read Amazon's TOS to know if the brief is safe to submit
- **Publishing instructions are step-by-step** — even buyers new to Brand Registry can follow them
- **Skill chaining proof**: the keywords ("ceramic pour over", "handmade", "paperless", etc.) come from the SEO Optimizer's cache and are woven naturally into headlines and body
