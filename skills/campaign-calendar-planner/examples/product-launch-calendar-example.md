**Brand:** {{brand_name}}
**Buyer request:** "Plan the launch of my new Mug Set — going live November 1"
**Region:** US
**Window:** October 10, 2026 – November 22, 2026 (~6 weeks: 3 weeks pre-launch, 3 weeks post-launch)

This example shows a launch-focused mini-calendar, distinct from a 90-day rolling calendar. The buyer wanted to plan a specific product launch with full pre-launch through post-launch coverage.

## Campaign structure: 1 main campaign with multi-phase send schedule

The mug set launch is the central campaign, with auxiliary BFCM coordination since November is involved.

### Phase 1: Waitlist build (October 10-25)

The pre-launch period focuses on growing the waitlist for the new product. Most sends are organic + paid, with light email pushes.

| Date | Channel | Send Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 10 | Organic Social | Studio sneak peek (Reel + IG carousel) — "what we're making" |
| Oct 12 | Email | "We're working on something" (waitlist signup CTA) |
| Oct 15 | Paid Ad | Meta + TikTok ads driving to waitlist signup |
| Oct 17 | Organic Social | More studio process content |
| Oct 22 | Organic Social | First teaser of the product (close-up only, full shot embargoed) |
| Oct 24 | Email | "It's almost ready" + first photos |
| Oct 25 | Paid Ad | Waitlist-conversion ads intensify |

### Phase 2: Pre-launch tease (October 26-31)

7-day countdown to launch.

| Date | Channel | Send Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Oct 26 | Email | "5 days to launch" |
| Oct 27 | Organic Social | Mug set hero shot reveal |
| Oct 28 | Email | "3 days to launch" |
| Oct 29 | Organic Social | UGC creator content (from skill #6 brief) |
| Oct 30 | Email | "Tomorrow at 9am" |
| Oct 31 | Email + SMS | "Going live in 12 hours" |

### Phase 3: Launch day (November 1)

The peak. All 5 channels active.

| Time | Channel | Send Topic |
|---|---|---|
| 9:00 | Email | LAUNCH — Mug Set is live, 24-hour waitlist early-access |
| 9:00 | SMS | Launch SMS to waitlist |
| 9:00 | Paid Ad | Meta + TikTok ads activate (full budget) |
| 9:00 | Site Update | Homepage hero, popup, announcement bar all live |
| 11:00 | Organic Social | IG Reel + IG feed post + TikTok |
| 14:00 | Organic Social | IG Story sequence (studio behind-the-scenes from this morning) |

### Phase 4: First week sustain (November 2-7)

Maintain momentum.

| Date | Channel | Send Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 2 | Email | "Public launch — yesterday's waitlist sold out; remaining stock now" |
| Nov 3 | Organic Social | Customer-content repost |
| Nov 4 | Email | Bundle option (mug set + River Stone pour-over) |
| Nov 5 | Organic Social | Founder Q&A in IG Stories |
| Nov 6 | Email | First review highlights |
| Nov 7 | Organic Social | Weekend "your morning with the mug set" UGC repost |

### Phase 5: Two-week reflection (November 14-15)

Customer-content content + transition to standard program.

| Date | Channel | Send Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 14 | Email | Customer-story feature (2 weeks in) |
| Nov 15 | Organic Social | "Two weeks of the Mug Set" recap content |

### Phase 6: BFCM bridge (November 16-22)

The launch campaign tapers as BFCM pre-tease begins. Mug Set is now part of the BFCM offer (no separate launch push).

| Date | Channel | Send Topic |
|---|---|---|
| Nov 16 | Email | Transition email — "next up: Black Friday" |
| Nov 22 | Email | BFCM pre-tease (the mug set is part of the BFCM sale) |

## Coordination notes the .docx flags

The brief documents which other skills should be invoked when, so the buyer doesn't miss the integration moments:

- **Pre-launch:** Run skill #1 (Product Description Writer) to draft listing copy for the new SKU. Run skill #2 (Listing SEO Optimizer) for product-level SEO. Run skill #3 (Landing Page Copywriter) for the dedicated launch landing page.
- **3 weeks pre-launch:** Run skill #6 (UGC Creator Brief Writer) for the creator content that lands in pre-launch tease and launch week.
- **2 weeks pre-launch:** Run skill #5 (Ad Creative Generator) for the launch ads. Pull hooks from skill #6's UGC cache so creator-side and ad-side hooks coordinate.
- **1 week post-launch:** Run skill #5 again for a second wave of ads with refreshed creative.
- **Transition to BFCM:** Coordinate with the Q4 BFCM calendar (see q4-bfcm-calendar-example.md).

These integration prompts appear inline in the .docx brief at the relevant phase.

## Total send count (excluding BFCM bridge)

- **22 emails** across the 6 phases
- **2 SMS** (launch day + day-before)
- **8 paid ad activations** (continuous through phases 1-4)
- **15 organic social posts** across Instagram + TikTok + Facebook
- **1 site update window** (Nov 1 through approximately Nov 14)

The launch concentrates effort in a 6-week window for high impact, rather than spreading across the full 90 days.

## Notes on this exemplar

- **Launch-focused mini-calendars are a real use case** — the skill supports them via the "custom date range" detection in step 2
- **The launch follows Playbook 2 from campaign-playbooks.md** with the 6-phase structure
- **Waitlist build is the highest-leverage pre-launch activity** — drives 5-10x better launch-day performance than no waitlist
- **The skill recommends invoking other plugin skills inline** — Product Description Writer, Listing SEO, Landing Page Copywriter, UGC Creator Brief, Ad Creative Generator — so the buyer doesn't miss the prep work
- **Phase 6 bridges to BFCM** — when November overlaps with BFCM, the calendar coordinates rather than running two unrelated campaigns
- **Per-phase send counts are tight** — launches that send too aggressively (15+ emails in 6 weeks) lose subscribers faster than they gain customers
