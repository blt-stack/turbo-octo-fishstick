# US Retail Holiday Calendar

Holidays and shopping events that drive e-commerce campaigns in the US. Each entry includes the date logic, retail relevance per category, and recommended send-timing for the campaign.

## How to use this file

The skill picks which holidays to build campaigns around based on:

1. **Category fit** — not every holiday warrants a campaign for every brand (a coffee brand doesn't need a Halloween campaign unless their voice fits it)
2. **Buyer preference** — if the buyer's brand voice is "anti-discount," skip pure-promo holidays in favor of brand-content ones
3. **Time horizon** — only include holidays that fall within the calendar window

Holidays are ordered chronologically through the year.

---

## January

### New Year's Day (January 1)
- **Type:** Cultural moment, low retail intensity
- **Retail relevance:** Low for most categories. Higher for fitness, wellness, supplements (resolution-driven).
- **Cadence:** 1 email "New Year, new collection" on Jan 1 or 2. Optional social posts.
- **Skip when:** the brand isn't aligned with the resolution narrative.

### MLK Day (3rd Monday)
- **Type:** Federal holiday, low retail
- **Retail relevance:** Low for most. Some retailers do MLK Day sales — controversial; skip unless the brand has explicitly chosen to do so for years.

## February

### Valentine's Day (February 14)
- **Type:** Major retail holiday
- **Retail relevance:** High for jewelry, beauty, gifts, food/drink (especially chocolate), date-night categories. Medium for apparel, home, fitness.
- **Cadence:**
  - Jan 28: Pre-launch tease ("Valentine's gift guide is coming")
  - Feb 1: Gift guide launch (email + paid ads)
  - Feb 5: Mid-campaign reminder
  - Feb 10: Last-shipping-day notice
  - Feb 12: 24-hour-left urgency
  - Feb 14: Day-of "still shopping?" (for buyers who shop the morning of)
- **Site updates:** Banner Feb 1-14 with "Valentine's gift guide" CTA. Homepage popup Feb 8-13.
- **Campaign duration:** 17 days
- **Skip when:** the brand's audience is overwhelmingly people who don't celebrate / are couples who skip retail Valentine's.

### Presidents' Day (3rd Monday in February)
- **Type:** Federal holiday + retail sale day
- **Retail relevance:** Medium for home, mattresses, appliances. Low for most other DTC.
- **Cadence:** 2-3 emails + light paid amplification over Presidents' Day weekend.

---

## March

### St. Patrick's Day (March 17)
- **Type:** Cultural moment, low retail intensity
- **Retail relevance:** Low for most. Medium for beverages, party supplies.
- **Cadence:** Optional single email/SMS Mar 16-17 if the brand fits.

### International Women's Day (March 8)
- **Type:** Cultural moment, brand-message-driven
- **Retail relevance:** Brand-fit driven. Many female-founded brands do a content / cause campaign rather than a discount.
- **Cadence:** 1 email + 2-3 social posts. Skip discounts; lean into content.

---

## April

### Easter (variable — late March or April)
- **Type:** Family holiday, moderate retail
- **Retail relevance:** High for kids' products, food, gifts. Low for most adult-targeted DTC.
- **Cadence:** Build a 7-day campaign starting Palm Sunday for relevant categories.

### Earth Day (April 22)
- **Type:** Cultural moment, brand-message-driven
- **Retail relevance:** Brand-fit driven (sustainability brands). Skip for non-aligned brands.
- **Cadence:** Content-led campaign in the week of Earth Day. Skip if it would feel performative.

---

## May

### Mother's Day (2nd Sunday in May)
- **Type:** Major retail holiday — top 5 of the year for many categories
- **Retail relevance:** High for jewelry, beauty, flowers, gifts, home, food. Medium-high for apparel.
- **Cadence:**
  - April 22: Pre-launch tease
  - April 28: Gift guide launch (email + paid ads)
  - May 1: Mid-campaign with bestsellers
  - May 5: Last-shipping-day notice (varies by carrier)
  - May 8: 48-hour-left
  - May 11 (Saturday before): Last-minute scramble
  - May 12 (Mother's Day): Quiet day, no sends
- **Duration:** 21 days
- **Site updates:** Mother's Day gift guide collection live April 28 – May 12. Banner + popup.

### Memorial Day (last Monday of May)
- **Type:** Federal holiday + major retail sale day
- **Retail relevance:** High for apparel, home, outdoor goods, summer-prep categories. Medium for most others.
- **Cadence:**
  - 5 days before: Pre-launch tease
  - Friday before: Launch
  - Memorial Day weekend (Sat-Mon): Full push + paid ads
  - Tuesday: Wrap-up

---

## June

### Father's Day (3rd Sunday in June)
- **Type:** Major retail holiday but smaller than Mother's Day
- **Retail relevance:** High for tools, tech, grilling, men's grooming, men's apparel. Medium for other gifts.
- **Cadence:** Same shape as Mother's Day, 14 days instead of 21.

### Pride Month (June)
- **Type:** Cultural / brand-aligned
- **Retail relevance:** Brand-fit driven. Skip for non-aligned brands or do it authentically with year-round support.
- **Cadence:** Content-led, ongoing throughout June if the brand fits.

### Juneteenth (June 19)
- **Type:** Federal holiday, brand-message driven
- **Retail relevance:** Brand-fit driven.

---

## July

### Independence Day / 4th of July (July 4)
- **Type:** Federal holiday + retail sale weekend
- **Retail relevance:** High for outdoor goods, BBQ/grilling, apparel (especially Americana). Medium for most others.
- **Cadence:**
  - July 1: Launch
  - July 2-4: Full weekend push
  - July 5: Wrap-up

### Amazon Prime Day (variable — usually mid-July)
- **Type:** Retail event (Amazon-driven)
- **Retail relevance:** High for sellers on Amazon. Medium for off-Amazon brands (who can run "anti-Prime-Day" or "shop direct, save more" campaigns).
- **Cadence:** Mirror Amazon's 2-day window with a brand-counter-campaign.

---

## August

### Back-to-School (peak weeks: August 5-25)
- **Type:** Cultural shopping moment
- **Retail relevance:** High for kids', school supplies, apparel (especially teen/college), tech. Medium-low for most DTC.
- **Cadence:** 3-week rolling campaign through August.
- The user's holiday selection didn't include cultural moments — only include this campaign if the buyer's category is BTS-relevant or asks for it.

---

## September

### Labor Day (1st Monday of September)
- **Type:** Federal holiday + retail sale weekend
- **Retail relevance:** Medium for most DTC. End-of-summer + fall-prep.
- **Cadence:**
  - Friday before: Launch
  - Labor Day weekend (Sat-Mon): Full push
  - Tuesday: Wrap-up

---

## October

### Halloween (October 31)
- **Type:** Cultural shopping moment
- **Retail relevance:** High for costumes, party, candy, kids', spooky-aesthetic brands. Low for most DTC.
- **Cadence:** Single-week campaign Oct 24-31 for relevant brands. Skip otherwise.

---

## November — BFCM TERRITORY

### Singles' Day (November 11)
- **Type:** Originally Chinese; growing in the US
- **Retail relevance:** Medium for US DTC; high for brands with Asian-American audiences.
- **Cadence:** Optional single-day push Nov 11.

### Thanksgiving (4th Thursday of November)
- **Type:** Cultural + soft retail
- **Retail relevance:** Low retail on Thanksgiving Day itself (most stores quiet) — it's the lead-up to Black Friday.
- **Cadence:** Pre-Thanksgiving "gratitude" content, then quiet Thanksgiving Day, then Black Friday.

### Black Friday (Friday after Thanksgiving) → Cyber Monday → Giving Tuesday
- **Type:** THE single largest e-commerce event of the year. Most stores do 20-40% of annual revenue in this 5-day window.
- **Cadence:** See `templates/campaign-playbooks.md` for the BFCM playbook (the most detailed one).
- **Build the BFCM campaign even when the buyer doesn't mention it.** The skill always includes BFCM if the calendar window covers any of late November.

---

## December

### Cyber Week (Dec 1 – Dec 5 typically)
- **Type:** Extension of BFCM
- **Retail relevance:** High for almost all DTC.
- **Cadence:** Continuation of BFCM with daily sends through Cyber Week.

### Hanukkah (variable — late Nov to late Dec, 8 nights)
- **Type:** Religious holiday
- **Retail relevance:** Medium for gift-driven brands. Often paired with Christmas campaigns.

### Last-Shipping-Day campaigns (mid-December, varies by carrier)
- **Type:** Urgency-driven scarcity (real urgency this time)
- **Cadence:**
  - 5 days before last-ship date: "Last day to ship in time for Christmas" email
  - 2 days before: "Cutting it close" urgency
  - Day-of: Final urgency
  - After: Pivot to digital gifts (gift cards) or "ships after Christmas" messaging

### Christmas Eve / Christmas Day (December 24-25)
- **Type:** Major holiday
- **Retail relevance:** Quiet sending — most subscribers don't want marketing emails on the actual holiday.
- **Cadence:** Light or skip entirely. Optional single warm "Merry Christmas" content email Dec 24 morning.

### Boxing Day (December 26 — UK/CA primarily, growing in US)
- **Type:** Post-holiday sale day
- **Retail relevance:** High in UK and Canada; growing in US.
- **Cadence:** Dec 26-30 post-holiday sale window.

### New Year's Eve (December 31)
- **Type:** Year-end content moment
- **Retail relevance:** Medium. End-of-year reflection content + gift-card / NYE resolution prep.
- **Cadence:** "Thank you for [year]" email Dec 28-30. NYE itself is quiet.

## Default US holiday set per category

The skill picks defaults based on the buyer's category. Examples:

### Beauty / skincare
- Valentine's Day (medium)
- Mother's Day (high)
- BFCM + Cyber Week (high)
- Last-shipping holiday push (medium)

### Apparel
- Mother's Day, Father's Day (medium)
- Memorial Day, Labor Day, July 4th (medium)
- BFCM + Cyber Week (very high)
- Last-shipping holiday push (high)

### Food / beverage
- Valentine's Day (medium for chocolate / treat categories)
- Mother's Day, Father's Day (medium)
- Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day (high — BBQ/grilling adjacent)
- Thanksgiving content (high — gratitude-driven)
- BFCM (high)
- Holiday gifting (high)

### Home / decor
- Mother's Day (high)
- Memorial Day, Labor Day (medium — home-refresh moments)
- BFCM (very high)
- Holiday gifting (high)

### Kids' / parenting
- Easter (high)
- Mother's Day, Father's Day (high)
- Back-to-school if requested (high)
- Halloween (medium)
- BFCM (very high)
- Holiday gifting (very high)

### Hobbyist / specialty (coffee, books, art supplies, etc.)
- Father's Day (medium — gift-driven)
- BFCM (high)
- Holiday gifting (high)
- 1-2 brand-specific tentpole campaigns per year
