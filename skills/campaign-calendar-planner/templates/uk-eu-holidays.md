# UK / Europe Retail Holiday Calendar

UK and European retail calendars differ from the US in several material ways:

- **UK Mother's Day falls in March** (Mothering Sunday — 4th Sunday of Lent, typically mid-to-late March), not May
- **Black Friday / Cyber Monday exists but is less dominant** than in the US; Boxing Day rivals or exceeds it in the UK
- **Bank Holidays** are major retail moments (May Day, Spring Bank, Summer Bank, etc.)
- **Christmas Eve and Boxing Day** are bigger retail events than in the US
- **No Thanksgiving** — the calendar between Halloween and Christmas is structured differently

This file covers UK + major EU markets (Germany, France, Spain, Italy, Netherlands). Specific local moments noted where they diverge significantly.

---

## January

### New Year's Day (January 1)
Universal. Same as US.

### Twelfth Night / Epiphany (January 6)
Mainly observed in continental Europe (Spain, Italy, France). Gift-giving moment in Spain (Three Kings Day). Skip for UK-only brands.

### January Sales (early-to-mid January)
**Type:** Post-holiday clearance — major retail tradition in the UK and EU.
**Retail relevance:** High across most categories. UK shoppers expect "January sales" with the same predictability as US Boxing Day.
**Cadence:** Jan 2 – Jan 15 sale window. Email + paid ad push.

---

## February

### Valentine's Day (February 14)
Universal. Slightly smaller in the UK than the US but still significant for jewelry, beauty, food/drink, gifts.

---

## March

### Mothering Sunday / UK Mother's Day (4th Sunday of Lent — variable, typically mid-to-late March)
**This is the key UK holiday difference.** US Mother's Day in May is NOT a retail moment in the UK.
**Type:** Major retail holiday in the UK and Ireland
**Retail relevance:** Same as US Mother's Day (high for jewelry, beauty, flowers, gifts, food, home)
**Cadence:** 21-day campaign window ending on Mothering Sunday.

### International Women's Day (March 8)
Cultural moment. Brand-fit driven across all regions.

### St. Patrick's Day (March 17)
Cultural — bigger in Ireland and UK than US for retail beverages, food.

---

## April

### Easter (variable — March or April)
Universal. In the UK/EU, Good Friday and Easter Monday are both Bank Holidays (4-day weekend).
**Type:** Major holiday, longer shopping window than US.
**Cadence:** Pre-Easter 7-10 day campaign for relevant categories.

### St. George's Day (April 23 — England)
Minor; mostly content-only for UK England-focused brands.

### King's Day / Koningsdag (April 27 — Netherlands)
Major national day in the Netherlands. Skip unless serving Dutch market specifically.

---

## May

### Early May Bank Holiday (1st Monday in May — UK)
**Type:** Bank Holiday, retail sale day
**Retail relevance:** Medium. Long weekend driver.
**Cadence:** Bank Holiday weekend campaign Friday–Monday.

### May Day / Labour Day (May 1 — most of EU)
Public holiday across France, Germany, Italy, Spain. Light retail (most shops closed in continental Europe).

### Spring Bank Holiday (last Monday in May — UK)
**Type:** Bank Holiday weekend
**Cadence:** Long-weekend campaign Friday–Monday.

### Mother's Day (continental EU equivalents):
- France: last Sunday in May
- Germany: 2nd Sunday in May
- Italy: 2nd Sunday in May
- Spain: 1st Sunday in May

For multi-EU brands, each market's Mother's Day campaign runs to its own date.

---

## June

### Father's Day (3rd Sunday in June — UK, most of EU)
Same date as US in the UK and most EU. Same retail relevance.

---

## July

### Bastille Day (July 14 — France)
French national holiday. Skip unless serving French market.

### Summer Sales (varies by country)
- UK: late June through July (concentrated end of season)
- France: legally-regulated dates (typically last Wednesday in June for 4 weeks)
- Germany: end of July through end of August (Sommerschlussverkauf)
- Italy: early July onwards

For multi-EU brands, summer sale messaging timed to each country's regulatory norms.

---

## August

### Summer Bank Holiday (last Monday in August — UK)
**Type:** Bank Holiday weekend
**Cadence:** Long-weekend campaign Friday–Monday.

### Ferragosto (August 15 — Italy)
Major holiday in Italy; most shops close. Skip Italy sends Aug 13-17.

---

## September

### Back-to-school (early September across UK/EU)
Most schools start early September (later than US). Retail campaigns run mid-August through mid-September.

---

## October

### Halloween (October 31)
Less of a retail driver than in the US, but growing. Medium relevance for costumes, candy, party. Low for most adult DTC.

### Diwali (variable — mid-October to mid-November)
Major in South Asian communities across UK + EU. Brand-fit driven.

---

## November

### Bonfire Night / Guy Fawkes (November 5 — UK)
Cultural, not retail-driven.

### Black Friday / Cyber Monday (4th Friday of November + following Monday)
**Adopted across UK and EU**, growing year-over-year. Now major in Germany, France, UK.
**Retail relevance:** High but less dominant than in the US (no Thanksgiving lead-up). Many UK/EU brands compress into Black Friday weekend rather than a week-long event.
**Cadence:** Same as US BFCM, slightly shorter (5-day window typically, not 8-day).

---

## December

### Sinterklaas / St. Nicholas (December 5-6 — Netherlands, Belgium, Germany)
Major gift-giving holiday in the Low Countries and parts of Germany. Toys, gifts, children's items.

### Christmas (December 25 + 26)
**Type:** Major holiday across UK/EU
**Key difference vs US:** Boxing Day (December 26) is a UK/EU retail event of similar magnitude to BFCM in some markets.
**Cadence:** Christmas campaign mirrors US, but extends through December 26-30 with Boxing Day promotions.

### Boxing Day (December 26 — UK, Canada, Australia, NZ)
**Type:** Major retail event — rivals or exceeds BFCM in the UK
**Retail relevance:** Very high across all DTC categories
**Cadence:**
- December 25 evening: Boxing Day preview email
- December 26-30: Full Boxing Day sale campaign with daily sends
- December 31: Year-end thank-you / Boxing Day wrap-up

### New Year's Eve (December 31)
Year-end reflection content. Light commercial activity.

## Notes on multi-region planning

When the buyer ships globally and the skill is asked for a multi-region calendar:

- **Each campaign in the calendar gets a "regions" attribute** specifying which markets it applies to
- **Conflicting dates are handled per-region** (e.g., UK Mother's Day in March + US Mother's Day in May are two separate campaigns, each gated to its region)
- **Send timezones** are documented per region (don't blast a UK-only campaign at 9am Pacific — that's 5pm London, past the optimal send window)

For Brexit-affected logistics: UK shipping cutoffs for international orders differ from EU; flag in the brief if relevant to the buyer's category.
