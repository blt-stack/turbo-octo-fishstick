# Campaign Playbooks

Reusable patterns for the most common campaign types. Each playbook specifies the typical campaign duration, the send-cadence skeleton (pre-launch through wrap-up), the channels involved, and category-specific tuning.

When the skill assembles the calendar, it picks the matching playbook per campaign and customizes for the buyer's brand voice + audience.

---

## Playbook 1: BFCM (Black Friday / Cyber Monday / Cyber Week)

The biggest e-commerce event of the year. Most DTC stores do 15-35% of annual revenue in this window.

**Duration:** ~14 days (Nov 22 – Dec 5, varies by year's calendar)

**Phase structure:**

| Phase | Days | Channels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-Black-Friday tease | Nov 22-25 | Email, organic social | Build anticipation, capture wishlist data |
| Black Friday launch | Nov 28 (or Black Friday Eve evening) | Email + SMS + paid ads + site banner + organic | Full activation |
| Black Friday peak | Black Friday day | All channels at peak intensity | Maximize day-of conversion |
| Saturday-Sunday continuation | Nov 29-30 | Email + paid ads + organic | Mid-event reminder |
| Cyber Monday peak | Dec 1 | All channels | Second peak |
| Cyber Week wind-down | Dec 2-5 | Email + paid ads (reduced) | Capture stragglers |
| Giving Tuesday content (optional) | Dec 2 | Email + organic | Optional brand-purpose moment |

**Total sends typical:** 8-15 emails + 4-8 SMS + 5-12 paid ad concepts + 10-20 organic posts + 4 site updates.

**Discount strategy:**
- Top-of-program offer: 25-40% off (the biggest of the year for most brands)
- Optional escalation: 25% on Friday, 30% on Cyber Monday, 40% on the last day
- Free shipping always included

**Category tuning:**
- **Apparel / accessories:** highest intensity. 20+ emails over 14 days isn't unusual.
- **Beauty / skincare:** very high. Bundle pushes work well.
- **Home / decor:** high. Big-ticket items convert here.
- **Food / drink:** high. Bundles + gifting positioning.
- **Hobbyist / specialty:** medium. Less of a discount culture; brand-content angle wins.
- **Premium / luxury:** counter-positioning — many premium brands skip BFCM intentionally. Skill respects this if buyer asks.

**Common pitfalls:**
- Underestimating subscriber fatigue — 15+ emails feels too much; tier by segment (engaged subscribers get all; less-engaged subscribers get a subset)
- Generic copy across all sends — variety wins
- Discounting more than necessary — test 25% before going to 40%
- No paid amplification — BFCM is when CPMs go up; competing requires paid ads

## Playbook 2: Product Launch

A new SKU or collection going live.

**Duration:** 10-21 days (depends on hype-building strategy)

**Phase structure:**

| Phase | Days | Channels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waitlist build | -21 to -8 | Organic social, paid ads, content | Generate signup interest pre-launch |
| Pre-launch tease | -7 to -3 | Email (to waitlist), organic, paid | Build anticipation |
| Launch day | 0 | All channels at peak | Convert waitlist + cold traffic |
| First week sustain | +1 to +7 | Email, paid ads, organic | Maintain momentum, social-proof content |
| Two-week reflection | +14 | Email + organic | Customer-story content, reviews push |

**Total sends typical:** 5-10 emails + 2-4 SMS + 5-8 paid ad concepts + 8-15 organic posts + 2-3 site updates.

**Pricing strategy:**
- Launch-week price (often the only discount the product ever sees)
- Waitlist-exclusive early access (24-48 hours before public)
- Free shipping for the launch window

**Coordination with other skills:**
- Run skill #1 (Product Description Writer) before launch — listing copy needs to be live
- Run skill #2 (Listing SEO Optimizer) before launch — keywords need to be in place
- Run skill #3 (Landing Page Copywriter) for the dedicated launch page
- Run skill #6 (UGC Creator Brief Writer) 14-21 days pre-launch to seed creator content
- Run skill #5 (Ad Creative Generator) 7 days pre-launch
- This calendar references all of those — the campaign brief calls out which skills to run when

**Common pitfalls:**
- Not building a waitlist — launches without one underperform 5-10x
- Launching during a high-noise window (during BFCM, on a major holiday) — gets buried
- Discounting the launch too steeply — sets a precedent for future pricing
- No post-launch storytelling — momentum dies if no customer-content follows in week 1

## Playbook 3: Restock Push

A previously-sold-out product is back in stock.

**Duration:** 5-10 days (faster than a launch — audience is already warm)

**Phase structure:**

| Phase | Days | Channels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waitlist notification | 0 | Email + SMS to waitlist | Convert pre-built demand |
| Public restock | +1 to +2 | Email + paid ads + organic + site banner | Reach beyond waitlist |
| Mid-window | +4 to +5 | Email + organic | Reminder |
| Last-call | +7 | Email + SMS | If stock is selling fast |

**Total sends typical:** 3-5 emails + 2-3 SMS + 2-3 paid ads + 3-5 organic posts.

**Coordination:**
- Skill #7's Back-in-Stock flow handles the waitlist trigger automatically — this campaign is the public-facing push beyond the waitlist

**Common pitfalls:**
- Not having a waitlist setup — restocks without waitlist sell slower
- Not enough urgency if stock is limited — "we have 200 left" beats "back in stock!"
- Over-promoting restocks for products that just sit on shelves — feels desperate

## Playbook 4: Flash Sale

A short, time-bounded discount push (24-72 hours).

**Duration:** 1-3 days

**Phase structure:**

| Phase | Time | Channels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surprise announcement | T-0 | Email + SMS + paid ads + site popup | Activation |
| Mid-sale reminder | T+24h (for multi-day) | Email + SMS | Reminder |
| Final hours | T+last 4 hours | Email + SMS + paid ads | Urgency |
| Post-sale | T+1 day | Email | Thank-you, soft return-to-full-price messaging |

**Total sends typical:** 2-4 emails + 2-3 SMS + 1-2 paid ads + site popup live during sale.

**Discount strategy:**
- Steeper than normal (30-50%) — flash sales need to feel special
- Often limited to specific products / collections (so it doesn't cannibalize full-price sales)

**Common pitfalls:**
- Running flash sales too often — they stop feeling flash, customers wait for the next one
- Sale runs longer than promised — kills future flash-sale credibility
- Generic "Flash Sale!" subject — be specific ("48 hours: 40% off the bestsellers")

## Playbook 5: Brand Anniversary / Birthday

The brand's founding date or a milestone (5,000 customers, 1 year in business, etc.).

**Duration:** 7-14 days

**Phase structure:**

| Phase | Days | Channels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reflection content | -7 to -3 | Email + organic | Origin story, year-in-review |
| Anniversary day | 0 | Email + SMS + site banner + organic | Celebration + special offer |
| Mid-week customer stories | +2 to +5 | Email + organic | Featured customer content |
| Wrap-up | +7 | Email | Thank-you |

**Total sends typical:** 3-5 emails + 1 SMS + 2-3 organic posts + site banner.

**Offer strategy:**
- Anniversary-specific discount code (e.g., "TURN3" for 3rd anniversary, 13% off)
- Limited-edition product if the brand has SKU capacity
- Free gift with purchase

**Content angle:**
- Lean heavily on brand story — anniversaries are NOT a heavy-promo moment
- Founder voice content
- Customer story features

**Common pitfalls:**
- Treating anniversary like a flash sale (too promotional) — undermines the brand moment
- Skipping customer-content (the celebration should include the customers)

## Playbook 6: Subscriber-Growth Campaign

Push to grow the email + SMS list (typically with a sign-up incentive).

**Duration:** 14 days

**Phase structure:**

| Phase | Days | Channels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site popup launch | 0 | Site popup + homepage banner | Capture sign-ups across traffic |
| Paid push | +1 to +14 | Paid ads (Meta + TikTok) | Drive new traffic to the popup |
| Organic push | +1 to +14 | Organic social | Reinforce the offer |

**Offer:**
- 10-15% off first order for sign-up
- Free shipping for sign-up
- Free downloadable content (guide, ebook, recipe collection)

**Total sends typical:** Site popup + 3-4 paid ad concepts + 5-8 organic posts. Email-program impact comes in the form of more subscribers entering the Welcome flow (skill #7).

## Playbook 7: Seasonal Tentpole (non-holiday)

A multi-week brand-driven seasonal campaign (e.g., "Slow Mornings of Summer," "Cozy Season Drop," "Fresh Start January").

**Duration:** 21-42 days

**Phase structure:**

| Phase | Days | Channels | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept launch | -7 to 0 | Email + paid ads + organic + site updates | Establish the seasonal narrative |
| Sustain content | +1 to +21 | Email (weekly) + organic (daily) + paid (ongoing) | Keep the narrative active |
| Wrap-up / transition to next season | -7 to 0 (from end) | Email + organic | Close the chapter |

**Total sends typical:** 3-5 emails + 8-15 organic posts + ongoing paid ads + 2-3 site updates.

**Discount strategy:**
- Usually no specific discount — this is a brand campaign, not a promo
- Optional: a tentpole collection at a small premium pricing (e.g., a limited-edition collection)

## How the skill picks playbooks

For each campaign in the calendar, the skill matches the campaign type to a playbook:

- Holiday-driven → use the holiday's recommended cadence (often a customized version of Playbook 1's BFCM shape, scaled down)
- Product launch (buyer specifies) → Playbook 2
- Restock (buyer or skill-generated) → Playbook 3
- Buyer-requested flash sale → Playbook 4
- Brand anniversary (from config) → Playbook 5
- List-growth (skill-suggested) → Playbook 6
- Tentpole / seasonal (buyer or skill-suggested) → Playbook 7

Each campaign's brief notes which playbook informed the structure, so the buyer understands the logic.
