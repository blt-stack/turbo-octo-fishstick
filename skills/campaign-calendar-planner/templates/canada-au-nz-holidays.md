# Canada / Australia / New Zealand Retail Holiday Calendar

These three markets each follow their own retail calendars. Canada's calendar is closer to the US than UK; Australia and NZ have a distinct Southern Hemisphere seasonal pattern that inverts much of the conventional retail rhythm.

This file covers all three markets together. Per-market notes are flagged.

---

## January

### New Year's Day (January 1) — all three
Universal.

### Australia Day (January 26 — AU)
**Type:** National holiday + retail moment
**Retail relevance:** Medium for apparel (summer outfits — it's mid-summer), food/drink (BBQ, beer), outdoor goods.
**Cadence:** Friday before through Australia Day Monday push.

### Auckland Anniversary Day (late January — NZ)
NZ regional holiday. Skip unless serving NZ Auckland market specifically.

### Boxing Day Sales Continuation (December 26 – mid-January)
**Type:** Continuation of Boxing Day clearance
**Retail relevance:** High across all three markets. The "January sales" tradition is strong.
**Cadence:** Continuation campaign through mid-January.

---

## February

### Waitangi Day (February 6 — NZ)
NZ national day. Low retail.

### Valentine's Day (February 14) — all three
Universal. Same retail relevance as US (high for jewelry, beauty, gifts).

---

## March

### Canberra Day (2nd Monday in March — AU Capital Territory)
Regional only.

### St. Patrick's Day (March 17)
Cultural moment; modest retail.

### Mother's Day (UK-style mid-March)
Not observed in Canada, AU, or NZ — they follow US-style May date.

---

## April

### Easter — all three
**Type:** Major holiday across all three markets. Good Friday + Easter Monday are public holidays in Canada, AU, NZ (4-day weekend).
**Retail relevance:** Medium-high for gifts, kids', food.
**Cadence:** 7-10 day pre-Easter campaign.

### ANZAC Day (April 25 — AU + NZ)
**Type:** National day of remembrance — solemn, not retail-driven
**Retail relevance:** Low. Many stores close. Brands should NOT run promotional content on ANZAC Day.
**Cadence:** Pause promotional sends April 25.

---

## May

### Mother's Day (2nd Sunday in May) — all three
**Same date as US** in Canada, AU, NZ.
**Retail relevance:** High (same as US).
**Cadence:** Same shape as US Mother's Day — 21-day campaign window ending Mother's Day.

### Victoria Day (last Monday before May 25 — Canada)
**Type:** Canadian federal holiday + retail sale weekend
**Retail relevance:** Medium for home, outdoor goods, summer-prep categories.
**Cadence:** Friday-through-Monday long-weekend campaign.

---

## June

### Father's Day (varies by market)
- **Canada + US:** 3rd Sunday in June (same date)
- **AU + NZ:** 1st Sunday in September (Southern Hemisphere — they observe in spring, not summer)

For multi-market brands, AU/NZ Father's Day runs as a separate September campaign.

### Queen's Birthday (2nd Monday in June — AU most states; 1st Monday in June — NZ)
**Type:** AU/NZ public holiday + retail weekend
**Retail relevance:** Medium for outdoor goods, home (winter-prep in Southern Hemisphere).

### National Indigenous Peoples Day (June 21 — Canada)
Cultural moment. Brand-fit driven.

---

## July

### Canada Day (July 1)
**Type:** Canadian national holiday + retail sale weekend
**Retail relevance:** High for Canadian brands. Equivalent of US July 4th in marketing intensity.
**Cadence:** Canada Day weekend campaign.

### July 4 (US Independence Day)
Not observed in Canada/AU/NZ. Skip for non-US markets.

---

## August

### Summer Bank Holiday equivalents
- AU: no equivalent
- NZ: no equivalent
- Canada: provincial civic holidays (1st Monday of August in most provinces) — medium retail relevance

---

## September

### Labour Day (1st Monday in September — Canada)
**Type:** Federal holiday + back-to-school + summer's end
**Retail relevance:** Medium-high for apparel, school, home.
**Cadence:** Long-weekend campaign + back-to-school overlap.

### Father's Day (1st Sunday in September — AU + NZ)
See Father's Day note above.

### Spring is here (AU + NZ)
September 1 marks spring in the Southern Hemisphere. Spring-collection launches happen here, not in March.

---

## October

### Thanksgiving (2nd Monday in October — Canada)
**Type:** Canadian national holiday
**Retail relevance:** Medium for food, home, family-gathering categories. Less retail-dominant than US Thanksgiving (which is the lead-up to BFCM) — Canadian Thanksgiving stands alone.
**Cadence:** Long-weekend campaign.

### Halloween (October 31)
Same as US — high for costumes, party, kids'. Low for most adult DTC.

### Labour Day (last Monday in October — NZ)
NZ public holiday + long weekend.

---

## November

### Diwali (variable — mid-October to mid-November)
Major in South Asian communities in all three markets.

### Black Friday / Cyber Monday — all three
**Adopted in all three markets**, with varying intensity:
- Canada: Strong, similar to US in scale
- Australia: Growing fast, now major
- NZ: Growing but smaller than AU
**Cadence:** Same as US BFCM. AU/NZ shoppers buy mostly summer items (BFCM is in early summer there, not winter).

### Remembrance Day (November 11 — Canada, AU, NZ + UK)
Solemn observance. **Pause promotional sends on November 11**.

---

## December

### Christmas (December 25) — all three
**In Australia and NZ, this is mid-summer.** Marketing should reflect summer Christmas (beach, BBQ, outdoor) — not winter snow imagery.

### Boxing Day (December 26) — Canada, AU, NZ
**Type:** Major retail event in all three markets
**Retail relevance:** Very high — rivals BFCM in many categories
**Cadence:**
- December 25 evening: Boxing Day preview
- December 26-31: Full Boxing Day sale campaign with daily sends
- January 1+: Continuation into January sales

This is the biggest single retail day in many Canadian / Australian / NZ brands' year.

### New Year's Eve (December 31)
Year-end content moment across all three markets.

## Southern Hemisphere seasonal inversion

A critical thing US/UK brand owners often miss when planning for AU/NZ:

| Season in Northern Hemisphere | Same months in AU/NZ |
|---|---|
| Winter (Dec-Feb) | Summer |
| Spring (Mar-May) | Autumn |
| Summer (Jun-Aug) | Winter |
| Fall (Sep-Nov) | Spring |

Implications for campaign messaging:
- **Christmas in AU/NZ** is summer. Beach Christmas, BBQ Christmas, not snow Christmas.
- **June-August** is the WINTER campaign window in AU/NZ. Cozy, indoor, warm.
- **Spring fashion drops** in AU/NZ happen in September, not March.
- **Back-to-school** in AU/NZ is late January / early February (school year matches calendar year).
- **Sundress / swimsuit / outdoor furniture** ad seasonality is inverted.

The skill flags these for multi-region brands so messaging stays appropriate.

## Default holiday set per category (Canada / AU / NZ)

### Beauty / skincare
- Valentine's Day (medium across all)
- Mother's Day (May for Canada; same for AU/NZ — high)
- BFCM (high)
- Christmas (very high)
- Boxing Day (very high)

### Apparel
- Easter long weekend (medium)
- Victoria Day (Canada — medium)
- Canada Day (high for Canadian brands)
- AU/NZ Father's Day in September (high for AU/NZ brands)
- BFCM (high)
- Boxing Day (very high)

### Outdoor / sport
- Australia Day (AU — high for outdoor / beach / summer)
- Canada Day (Canada — high)
- Queen's Birthday (AU/NZ — medium)
- Christmas (summer in AU/NZ — high for outdoor)
- Boxing Day (very high)

### Kids' / parenting
- Easter (high)
- Mother's Day, Father's Day (per region — high)
- Back-to-school (early Feb in AU/NZ; late Aug in Canada)
- Christmas + Boxing Day (very high)
