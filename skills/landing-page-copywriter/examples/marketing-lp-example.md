## What's distinctive about a marketing LP vs PDP

- No top nav — single-purpose page
- Risk reversal block is mandatory (not on PDP)
- Objection-handling block is mandatory (PDP usually defers to FAQ)
- Longer copy total (more sales work to do for cold traffic)
- PASTOR or PAS framework rather than JTBD or BAB
- FAQ is conversion-focused (returns, shipping, price justification), not product trivia
- Featured reviews lean harder on outcome/transformation than typical PDP reviews
